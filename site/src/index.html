The pause
between marks.

At 06:40, the cursor stops at the end of a line. For a moment the screen is less a machine than a page: a dark mark, a pale ground, a little room on either side. We notice the word before we notice its letters.

In a terminal, the i and the m each get one cell. On this page, the narrow letter and the broad one take the room they need. Set the same sentence both ways and listen to where your eye slows down.
