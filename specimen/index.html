Codex Sans

UI + everyday web

Good interface typography should disappear into the work. Open counters, distinct silhouettes, and a generous x-height keep instructions clear even when the screen is busy.

Bold establishes structure. Italic adds a quieter second voice. Neither needs to shout.

Regular — The quick brown fox, 0123456789
Italic — Thoughtful emphasis without noise
Bold — Actions, states, and hierarchy
Bold Italic — Urgent, but still composed

Codex Serif

Editorial + long-form

Evidence changes the texture of a decision. What began as a plausible explanation becomes a durable conclusion only after the behavior has been reproduced, observed, and written down.

The serif face is meant for that slower register: reports, research notes, design narratives, and documentation with enough room to breathe.

Readable at ordinary sizes. This paragraph is set at sixteen pixels, not poster scale. Letterforms remain conventional, punctuation carries its weight, and mixed-case text forms a steady line.

“Clarity is not simplification for its own sake; it is the removal of accidental difficulty.”

Diacritics: À É Ö ñ æ œ ß. Symbols: € £ ¥ © ™. Dashes—short and long—quotes “curved,” ellipses… all present.
